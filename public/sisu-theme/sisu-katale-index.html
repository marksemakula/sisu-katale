About Us

Our passion is childhood,
and we’re in kindergarten

Scooby Doo International school Uganda is a prominent institution located in Kampala, offering a range of educational services, which includes Daycare and Nursery education. It is known for its holistic, child-centered learning approach and provides a safe and child-friendly environment. The school also emphasizes the importance of parental involvement and offers various activities and events to engage both children and their parents. The school's age group is 2 - 5 years.
