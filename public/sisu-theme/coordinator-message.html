Welcome Message

Welcome to our Preschool family

At Scooby Doo International school Uganda, we believe that the early years are the foundation of a child's lifelong journey of learning. Our Mission is to Provide quality holistic education that fosters creativity, critical thinking and collaboration to produce global citizens.

We focus on fostering creativity, independence, and kindness through play-based learning and hands-on experience.

Our dedicated staff work closely with families to ensure that each child's unique strengths are recognized and encouraged.

Together, we are building not only strong learners but also compassionate individuals who will thrive in school and beyond.

- Mrs. Nabyonga Sarah
