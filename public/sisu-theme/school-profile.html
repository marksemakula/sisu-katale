School Profile

Scooby Doo International
School Uganda

Scooby Doo International School Uganda was established in 2013 and is located in Kikajjo, off Katale Road, in the Seguku area of Wakiso District, Uganda. The school is situated in a quiet, traffic-free environment that provides a safe and conducive atmosphere for young children's learning and development.

We are part of the Gombe Educational Services (GES) family of schools, and we have grown into a respected institution known for nurturing children's academic, social, emotional and physical development through holistic learning.

All essential learning materials are provided by the school, allowing every child to arrive ready to learn.

Unique Programs

Child-centred programmes
that set us apart

  • Play-based learning following the NCDC and EYFS curricula.
  • A strong emphasis on Jolly Phonics, early reading and writing skills.
  • Ballet, dance and drama, music, soccer, chess, piano, Kids for Peace and creative art.
  • Swimming and water-play activities.
  • Life-skills development, including communication, leadership and teamwork.
  • Talent identification and nurturing.
  • Parent participation through presentations, meetings and school events.
  • Character education based on values of respect, integrity, teamwork, excellence and being God-fearing.
